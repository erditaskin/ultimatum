## Review limits

- Premium star tokens, resource values, rewards and character appearances are proposals, not accepted business rules.
- NPC order portraits and decorative typography require separate asset and accessibility review.
- The screen generator approximated some previously approved ore silhouettes. Implementation must use the individually approved sprites rather than extracting approximations from this screen.
- Blackrock stage five appears to levitate. Whether this magical behavior is accepted remains open.
- This screen establishes visual direction, not verified device layout or measured touch targets. Exact layout, sprite placement, typography, safe areas and effects need a measured specification after approval.
